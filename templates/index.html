Scheduling rules used
  • Store hours: Mon–Sat 8:00 AM–8:00 PM, Sun 8:00 AM–5:00 PM.

  • 2nd Wednesday of the month is also an all-hands day — Clare, Jem, and Von are all pulled in alongside Jha, Macky, and Joshua.

  • No one works more than 6 consecutive days — a rest day is inserted automatically before a 7th straight day.
  • Opening/Closing runs in blocks — up to 4 straight days on the same shift type before switching, instead of flipping day to day.
  • Overlap days (a 3rd sales person on Opening or Closing) are spread roughly weekly rather than bunched at month-end, so Clare/Jem/Von reach their guaranteed monthly day counts.
  • Days off are grouped into consecutive pairs where possible.
