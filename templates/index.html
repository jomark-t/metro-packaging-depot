        Scheduling rules used
        • Store hours: Mon–Sat 8:00 AM–8:00 PM, Sun 8:00 AM–5:00 PM.
        • Sales shifts: Opening 8:00 AM–5:00 PM, Closing 11:00 AM–8:00 PM.
        • Managers — fixed Monday–Saturday, Sunday off. Alternate a full week on Opening then a full week on Closing (continuing seamlessly across month boundaries), covering one of the two slots those days. Two or more managers are staggered so they never both open the same week. Paid a fixed monthly salary rather than a daily rate — see Payroll notes below.
        • Sales staff — work to their monthly target (21 days each for Jem and Von), filling whichever Opening/Closing slots the manager and part-timers aren't already covering. Anyone without a target set is treated as full-time Mon–Sat.
        • Part-time sales — permanent Wed 8AM–5PM (Opening), Fri 8AM–12NN assist, plus a share of the Sundays. A single part-timer works most Sundays but always keeps at least 2 a month to rest; several take turns instead.
        • Sundays are capped at 2 people total on Opening (8AM–5PM); managers don't work regular Sundays.
        • 1st Sunday of the month is inventory day — all 6 staff work, including Clare. The following Monday is automatically her compensatory day off in lieu of that Sunday.
        • 2nd Wednesday of the month is also an all-hands day — Jem and Von are both pulled in alongside Clare (already working her normal Wed shift), Jha, Macky, and Joshua.
        • Machine Operators — fixed 9:00 AM–6:00 PM, Mon–Fri only. Exactly one Printer works each day and everyone else that day is a Checker. Printer days are shared out a week at a time, weighted toward whoever has printed least so far: with the usual two operators that means one does 3 days Printer / 2 Checker and the other the reverse, alternating week to week.
        • No one works more than 6 consecutive days — a rest day is inserted automatically before a 7th straight day (Jem/Von; Clare's fixed Sunday-off already keeps her under this, aside from the inventory-day exception above).
        • Opening/Closing runs in blocks — up to 4 straight days on the same shift type before switching, instead of flipping day to day.
        • Overlap days (an extra sales person on Opening or Closing) are spread roughly weekly rather than bunched at month-end, so everyone reaches their guaranteed monthly day counts.
        • Hiring and archiving are picked up automatically — staff are scheduled by their category, not by name, so regenerating a month includes anyone new and drops anyone archived.
        • Payroll — Clare: fixed ₱18,000/month, split evenly across the two semi-monthly cutoffs. Each unreported Mon–Sat day deducts ₱18,000÷26; that's waived 1-for-1 by any extra Sunday actually worked that cutoff (the mandatory inventory Sunday doesn't count toward this, since it's already balanced by her automatic Monday off).
        • Paid Time Off counts as a full paid day in payroll, same as a normal worked day.
        • Days off are grouped into consecutive pairs where possible.
